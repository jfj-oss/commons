Good to know

Linux: make the AppImage executable (file properties, or chmod +x), then run it. Calls open in your browser; use Chrome, Brave, Edge or Firefox.

macOS: the app is not notarized. Drag it to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time.

Windows: unzip it and run Commons.exe (keep the two files together). It is not signed: if SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Your passphrase never leaves your computer and nobody can reset it. Save the recovery key the app shows you.
