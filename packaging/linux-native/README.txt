mega (native) for Linux

  ./install.sh        installs for your user (no root): ~/.local/bin/mega-gpui, icons, app-menu entry
  ./uninstall.sh      removes them again; your account is left alone

mega uses the same account folder as the older Megatron app (~/.local/share/app.commons.chat). Only one of the two can
have the account open at a time; the second one says so. It needs Vulkan (a working graphics driver) to open a window.
